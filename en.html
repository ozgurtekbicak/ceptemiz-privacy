CepTemiz Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

In short: CepTemiz collects no personal data and sends nothing off your device. Your photos and videos stay on your phone.

Data we collect

None. CepTemiz has no accounts, sign-up, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting tools. The app does not connect to the internet and does not send data to any server.

Photo library access

CepTemiz asks for access to your photo library so it can show your photos and videos as cards and help you decide which ones to keep or delete. This access:

  • is used only with your permission and only on your device,
  • never uploads or shares your photos, videos or their metadata (location, date, etc.),
  • does not download originals stored in iCloud; only previews already on the device are shown.

An item is deleted only after you confirm it. Deletion goes through the standard iOS confirmation dialog, and deleted items are moved to the “Recently Deleted” album in the Photos app.

You can change or revoke this permission at any time in Settings › CepTemiz › Photos.

Information stored on your device

To work properly, the app stores the following only in its own storage on your device:

  • your theme and language preferences,
  • which items you chose to keep or delete (only opaque item identifiers provided by the system; no images, names or content),
  • whether you have seen the introduction screens.

This information never reaches us and is removed when you delete the app. iOS may include app data in your device’s own iCloud or computer backup; that backup is kept in your Apple account and we have no access to it.

Third parties

CepTemiz does not share data with any third party and does not track you.

Children’s privacy

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and the effective date above will be updated.
